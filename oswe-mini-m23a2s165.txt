## 🔍 What I changed
- Bottlenecks found:
  - COUNT(*) on `users` executed on every paginated request (heavy on large table).
  - N+1 when resolving follower → user rows.
  - Missing composite index for follower lookups.
- Concrete fixes:
  1. Replace per-request COUNT(*) with O(1) lookup from `counters.users`.  
  2. Replace N+1 follower loads with a single JOIN.  
  3. Add composite index on `(user_id, follower_id)` (non-destructive).
- No pagination semantics, authentication, ORM usage, or response shapes were changed.
